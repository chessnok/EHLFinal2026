FAQ
What teams ask us at the Tacto desk, answered once for everyone. The full rules are in the brief and the API docs in the kit repo.

How is the score calculated - for one negotiation and for the whole tournament?
Every case scores 0 to 1: the share of the possible saving you captured. In the Live Final each wave scores 0-100, and your points are the sum of the five waves - up to 500.

One negotiation
Your deal becomes one number in €. The term sheet you accept is priced as its total cost C: what it costs to cover your need over the case's horizon, at the case's published rates.
Price    committed units × unit price
Uncovered need    need the deal does not cover, at the fallback price, plus the category goal's risk on it
Payment    interest for paying earlier than your reference payment days
Freight, lead time    freight by Incoterm; € per day beyond the lead time you need
Quality, tooling, index    expected defects minus chargebacks; tooling; an index clause along the case's index path, plus its risk
Supplier's health    strategic and bottleneck goals only: € for every margin point you leave the supplier below a sustainable margin
The score compares your deal with the opening and the best possible deal.
score =
Copen − Cdeal
Copen − Cbest
clipped to 0…1
Copen
the cheapest opening quote in the case (one that keeps your must-haves).
Cbest
the best package any supplier in the case would ever accept, with every valid lever applied - or your alternative, if that is cheaper. Fixed per case: good arguments raise your score, they never move the target.
Edge cases. No deal scores your alternative (0 if there is none) - some cases are best left without a deal. A broken must-have scores 0. A deal worse than the opening scores 0.
Example · a leverage case
need 4,000 a year for 2 years · interest 8 % · reference 60 days · 3 % fallback risk
Copen €347,904
your deal €332,800
Cbest €324,224
Terms    C
Copen    €43.20, 30 days: 8,000 × 43.20 + 30 days of early payment at 8 %    €347,904
Your deal    €41.60, 60 days: 8,000 × 41.60    €332,800
Cbest    €40.80, 90 days: 8,000 × 40.80 − 30 days of interest saved    €324,224
Score    (347,904 − 332,800) / (347,904 − 324,224)    0.64
Trap: the same €41.60 for only 12 months covers half your need. The rest counts at the fallback price (say €46.00) plus 3 %: C = €355,920 - worse than the opening, score 0.

Every practice result shows Copen, Cbest, your C and its breakdown exactly. arena_kit.total_cost computes C on your laptop.

The tournament
Practice · now until Sun 12:00
Your best score per hosted practice case counts. The practice board shows your mean over the 10 hosted cases. Practice does not rank - it is for learning.

Live Final · Sun 12:00
5 themed waves, 8 minutes each, one attempt per case, every team at once.
Wave score = mean of the wave's case scores × 100 (0-100, one decimal). A case still open at the cap, or never opened, counts as no deal.
Points = the sum of your wave scores, up to 500. Every case weighs the same.
Ties: more deals, then fewer rounds. The top 10 pitch to the Tacto jury from 13:00.
€ saved on the board is a side number: the opening's cost minus your deal's (or your alternative's). It does not rank - € favours the big cases, points weigh every case the same.

What counts - the euros we save, or the score in percent?
The score ranks. In the final we show both numbers, but your place comes from your average performance per negotiation, not from the euros.

The score is your negotiating skill. Each case scores 0 to 1: the share of the possible saving you captured. Each wave is the mean of its cases × 100; your points are the sum of the waves.
Every negotiation weighs the same. A €40,000 saving on a big contract and a €4,000 saving on a small part can show the same skill. In euros the big case would drown out the small one; in points it doesn't.
€ saved is shown too. The board and the results page show what you saved: the opening's total cost minus your deal's, over the case's horizon. It's real money and worth a mention in your pitch - it just doesn't decide the ranking.
What can we run locally, and what do we have to deploy?
Nothing has to be deployed. Your agent is a client that calls our API - it needs no server and no public URL.

On your machine: your agent, the kit, the data package (SQLite and documents), the cost function, the MCP server over the data, and arena-sim - the arena's API locally, with a simple scripted supplier: no key, no quota, no clock.
Only on arena.tacto.ai: the real supplier bots - practice, sandbox and the final. They do not ship.
For the final: your agent runs and polls from before 12:00 until the last wave has closed. A cloud VM is safer than a laptop on venue Wi-Fi (sleep, dropped connections). We don't run your code: you submit your repo and commit hash on ehl.gg and run that commit yourself.
Can we get a good score without paying a lot for tokens?
Yes. The suppliers' model costs are ours - you pay only for your buyer agent's model, and a good agent needs fewer tokens than you think.

Math in code, not in the model. total_cost prices any term sheet for free. Let the model choose arguments and write the e-mail.
Prepare once, reuse. The data changes once (Sunday 08:00 release). Build a short dossier per supplier from the data, cache it, reuse it in every run.
Mix models. A strong model for the plan of a case, a small one for the writing. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works, a local model (e.g. Ollama) too.
Test plumbing without a model. The kit's naive agent on arena-sim costs nothing.
Threads are short. At most 10 of your messages per supplier by default - a case is a handful of calls, not thousands.
