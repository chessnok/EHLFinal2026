How to keep it honest

Use a pool of opponents. Include a cooperative one, a hardball one, one that walks away early, one with deadline pressure, and one that tries to manipulate or prompt-inject your agent. If possible, use different models, and randomize the opponents’ reservation prices.
Run several seeds per matchup. Report the mean, the worst case and the deal rate, not just the mean.
Accept a change only if it wins across most opponents, not just on the overall average.
Spend 2–3 public submissions on calibration, not optimization. Submit two variants that differ a lot locally and check that the public board ranks them the same way. If it does, you can trust the harness. If not, fix the simulator before optimizing further.
Use public transcripts if you can get them. Real opponent behavior is more useful than the opponent’s prompt; build your personas to imitate it.
